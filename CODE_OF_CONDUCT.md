# Code of conduct

Be direct about the work and decent to the people doing it.

Criticism of code, design, and measurement is the point of this project; the
design document says it is meant to be attacked. Attack claims, not people.
Harassment, personal insults, discrimination, and sustained hostility have
no place here, in issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere else the
project convenes.

Disagreements about technical direction get settled by evidence where
evidence is possible, and by the maintainers where it is not. Being right
does not license being cruel, and seniority does not exempt anyone from
this document.

Report conduct problems to the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports
are read by a human, kept confidential, and acted on; the response scales
from a conversation to removal from project spaces, at the maintainers'
judgment.
